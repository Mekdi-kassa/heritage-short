## 3. Platform AI Labelling & Transparency Plan

To prevent digital deception and uphold creative honesty, the following three-tier labeling plan is strictly enforced:

### 1. In-Video AI End Card (Shot 10)
- The final 7.5 seconds of the film feature a high-contrast, permanent attribution end card:
  ```
  የደሴው ሬዲዮ ጠጋኝ - The Radio Craftsman of Dessie
  Crafted with AI: Gemini 3.8 Flash, Wan 2.2, ACE-Step 1.5, FFmpeg
  Dedicated to the builders and storytellers of Ethiopia.
  ```

### 2. Social Platform Disclosure Toggles
- **YouTube / Shorts**: The `"Altered or synthetic content"` toggle is enabled on upload, adding YouTube's official *"Sound or visuals were significantly edited or digitally generated"* badge.
- **TikTok & Instagram Reels**: The native `"AI-generated content"` label toggle is switched ON prior to publication.

### 3. Provenance & Watermark Integrity
- No watermarks, SynthID signals, or C2PA provenance metadata generated by Gemini or FLUX were stripped, spoofed, or masked. Containers maintain standard MP4 provenance atom structures.

## 4. Cultural Representation & Privacy Safeguards

- **Indigenous Respect**: Traditional attire (Gabi, Netela) and musical instruments (Masenqo, Krar, Washint) are represented with historical accuracy, avoiding caricature or stereotypical distortions.
- **Data Privacy**: No home street addresses, private telephone numbers, or sensitive government credentials exist anywhere in the code, prompts, or logs.
